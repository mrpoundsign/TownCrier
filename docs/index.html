About TownCrier

TownCrier is an open-source Windows and Linux desktop application that reads livestream chat and stream alerts aloud using local neural text-to-speech (TTS), or serves as a lightweight, centralized multi-platform chat aggregator and event logger.

Audio routes directly to any selected playback device—such as a virtual audio cable, PipeWire/PulseAudio sink, or dedicated monitor output—allowing OBS Studio to capture TTS on an isolated audio track. Everything runs completely offline on your PC with zero cloud subscriptions, external servers, or character limits.

Key Features

  • Offline Neural Voice Packs: Local text-to-speech powered by Kokoro-82M, Supertonic, Kitten, and Piper ONNX models running directly on your CPU or GPU (DirectML).
  • Multi-Platform Stream Aggregation: Unified live chat monitoring and alerts for YouTube Live, Twitch, Kick, and TikTok.
  • Optional YouTube Live Moderation: Streamers can optionally connect their YouTube account via secure Google OAuth PKCE to review chat and perform moderation actions (deleting messages, timing out, or banning users) directly from the unified log.
  • Zero Cloud Telemetry or Servers: TownCrier operates entirely on your local machine. Your stream credentials, chat events, and OAuth tokens never touch any developer server.
  • Instant Panic Mute: Hardware emergency stop hotkey (Ctrl+Shift+M or Esc) to silence audio buffers in less than 50 microseconds.
